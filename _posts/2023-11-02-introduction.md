My name is Akhil and I am an embedded systems engineer.
<br><br>
I have nine years of professional software engineering experience, spanning production-scale distributed systems (Ruby, JavaScript, Python) and embedded firmware (C, C++) across IoT applications in the health wellness, smart home, and farming automation spaces. More recently, my work has centered on ARM Cortex-M bare-metal and RTOS-based firmware, drivers, BSPs, state machine design, and HIL testing.
<br><br>
I am primarily interested in electromechanical systems engineering, applying structured failure analysis and fault-tolerant design principles to meet safety-critical and performance targets. To this end, I can operate across the stack: from real-time embedded firmware and motor/actuator control up through state estimation and closed-loop control.
<br><br>
Academically, I have a B.S. in Mechanical Engineering and an M.S. in Electrical & Computer Engineering from Rutgers University. As part of the Rutgers Swarm Intelligence Lab, I led the design of a novel electromagnetic actuator system providing closed-loop trajectory control of a swarm of magnetic payloads, mimicing live zebrafish. This work has since been [published](http://ieeexplore.ieee.org/document/11400697) in the IEEE: Transactions on Control Systems Technology Journal.
